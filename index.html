Privacy Policy

DataCore AI stores account information needed to provide the service, including your email address, plan, usage counters, and chat history. Google sign-in may also provide your name and profile image.

Uploaded documents and images are processed to answer your request. Temporary files used for certain video operations are deleted after processing. Payment card details are handled on the payment provider's hosted checkout and are not stored by DataCore AI.

Payment records may include invoice IDs, payment IDs, status, plan, amount and subscription dates. You can clear chat history from My Account and can delete a non-owner account from the service.

AI and engineering content

AI output can be incorrect. Engineering and CAD output is conceptual assistance and must be checked and approved by a qualified engineer before field use.
